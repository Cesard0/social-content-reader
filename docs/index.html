The decision

A subtitle error shouldn’t discard usable media. But partial evidence must stay labeled partial.

Continue downloader errors, check what was actually saved, and preserve the error status. Narrow subtitle requests; allow local speech transcription when subtitles are unavailable.

Observed recovery

A later live retry saved the media. A fresh chat reused the cache and inspected all 12 frames.

Regression check

A simulated subtitle failure confirms usable media survives and remains marked partial.
